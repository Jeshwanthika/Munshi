# Indian (domestic) clients: UPI receipts, TDS and GST
Sources: taxguru.in, studycafe.in and busy.in TDS rate charts for Tax Year 2026-27 under Section 393 of the Income-tax Act 2025; keka.com guide to Section 194J / 393(1); xflowpay.com and skydo.com GST guides for freelancers; clevercoins.org on GST for IT services. Checked 10 Nov 2026.

## Do payments from Indian clients count?
Yes. Income tax applies to all your freelance receipts, whether they come from a foreign client through PayPal or from an Indian client through UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe), IMPS or NEFT. UPI and bank transfers are digital receipts, so they do not affect the 5% cash test of the presumptive scheme.

## Why PayPal can't collect from Indian clients
PayPal does not support domestic payments in India, so Munshi cannot send a PayPal invoice to an Indian client. Indian clients usually pay by UPI or bank transfer. Munshi can draft the invoice text and a UPI payment request, and counts the payment when you import your bank statement.

## TDS deducted by Indian business clients
When an Indian business pays you professional fees above Rs 50,000 in a tax year, it must deduct TDS (tax deducted at source) and pay you the rest. Under the Income-tax Act 2025 this is Section 393(1), which replaced old Section 194J. The rate is 10% for fees for professional services and 2% for fees for technical services. If you have not given your PAN, the rate is 20%. Individual clients paying for personal purposes generally do not deduct TDS.

## TDS is your tax already paid
Count the GROSS fee (amount received plus TDS) as your receipt. The TDS deducted is a credit against your income tax for the year: it reduces the advance tax you still owe and can be refunded if it is more than your final tax. Check that it appears in your Form 26AS / Annual Information Statement (AIS).

## GST for Indian clients
Services to Indian clients are not exports. Once your total turnover (Indian plus foreign clients) crosses Rs 20 lakh in a financial year, you must register for GST and charge 18% GST to Indian clients: CGST + SGST (9% + 9%) when the client is in your state, or 18% IGST when the client is in another state. Exports to foreign clients stay zero-rated under LUT. Below the threshold, registration is optional.
