# Advance tax for freelancers (Tax Year 2026-27)
Sources: Income Tax Department FAQ; taxguru.in practitioner guide to advance tax under the Income-tax Act 2025; cleartax.in advance tax page. Checked 9 Nov 2026.

## Do I have to pay advance tax?
Yes, if your estimated tax for the year, after TDS/TCS, is Rs 10,000 or more (Section 404 of the 2025 Act, old Section 208). Freelancers paid by foreign clients usually have no TDS, so the whole tax is advance tax.

## Due dates if you use the presumptive scheme
Taxpayers under the presumptive scheme (Section 58) can pay the entire advance tax in one instalment on or before 15 March of the tax year. For Tax Year 2026-27 that is 15 March 2027. Tax paid by 31 March is still treated as advance tax.

## Due dates for everyone else
Cumulative instalments: 15% by 15 June, 45% by 15 September, 75% by 15 December and 100% by 15 March. For Tax Year 2026-27 these dates fall in June, September and December 2026 and March 2027.

## Interest for missing it
Missing or underpaying advance tax attracts simple interest of about 1% per month on the shortfall under the interest provisions of the 2025 Act (old Sections 234B and 234C). Paying on time avoids it.

## How to pay
On the income-tax e-filing portal use e-Pay Tax, choose Income-tax Act 2025, payment type Advance Tax, and Tax Year 2026-27.
