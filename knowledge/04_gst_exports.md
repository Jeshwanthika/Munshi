# GST on export of services for freelancers
Sources: xflowpay.com and skydo.com GST guides for freelancers; incorpx.io guide to export of services (Section 16 IGST Act, Section 2(6)); taxguru.in on LUT under Rule 96A. Checked 9 Nov 2026.

## Do I need GST registration?
GST registration becomes mandatory when your aggregate turnover from services crosses Rs 20 lakh in a financial year (Rs 10 lakh in special category states). Export income counts towards this threshold. Below it, registration is optional, even if all clients are abroad.

## Is GST charged on foreign clients?
Export of services is a zero-rated supply under Section 16 of the IGST Act: 0% GST on qualifying export invoices. Zero-rated is not the same as exempt. Once you cross the threshold you must register even if all your work is exported.

## Conditions for a service to count as an export
Under Section 2(6) of the IGST Act: the supplier is in India, the recipient is outside India, the place of supply is outside India, payment is received in convertible foreign currency (or INR where RBI permits), and supplier and recipient are not just establishments of the same person.

## LUT: exporting without paying IGST
A registered exporter files a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) on the GST portal in Form GST RFD-11 (Rule 96A of the CGST Rules). It is valid for one financial year and must be renewed every year, ideally in April. Without an LUT you pay IGST on the export and claim a refund later. Export invoices under LUT should say "Supply meant for export under LUT without payment of IGST".

## Returns
A registered freelancer files GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B every period, declaring zero-rated export turnover even when no tax is payable.

## Indian clients
Services to Indian clients are not exports; once registered you charge 18% GST to them.
