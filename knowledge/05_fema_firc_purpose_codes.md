# Receiving foreign payments: purpose codes, FIRC/FIRA and PayPal
Sources: winvesta.in, wisemonk.io and earnfromhomeindia guides to receiving international payments in India (2026); Business Standard report on PayPal online FIRC. Checked 9 Nov 2026.

## Purpose codes
Every inward foreign remittance is tagged with an RBI purpose code that says what the money is for. Common codes for freelancers: P0802 for software consultancy and implementation (software, web and app development), and P0899 for other business services such as design, writing or marketing. Munshi puts the purpose code on every invoice note.

## FIRC and FIRA
A Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC), or its newer advice form FIRA/e-FIRA, is proof from an authorised bank that foreign currency came into India. It supports export-of-services treatment under GST and is useful when filing income tax. Keep one for every foreign receipt.

## PayPal in India
Since April 2021 PayPal does not support domestic INR payments in India, so every PayPal receipt for an Indian account is international. PayPal moves the funds to your Indian bank account in INR. PayPal digitised its FIRC application process for Indian sellers and freelancers.

## Fees to watch
Cross-border receipts carry a transaction fee plus a currency-conversion margin. For taxes, record the gross amount the client paid as your receipt and treat fees as business expenses.
