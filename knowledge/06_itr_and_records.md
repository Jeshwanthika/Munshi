# Filing and record keeping for freelancers
Sources: patronaccounting.com ITR guide for professionals (2026); taxguru.in on Section 58 and books of account (Section 62). Checked 9 Nov 2026.

## Which return
Freelancers using the presumptive scheme with total income up to Rs 50 lakh have generally used ITR-4 (Sugam). Others use ITR-3. Check the forms notified for the year you file, because forms are being updated for the 2025 Act.

## Audit
A tax audit (Section 63 of the 2025 Act, old 44AB) can be required if professional gross receipts exceed Rs 50 lakh and the presumptive scheme is not used, or if you declare profit below the presumptive rate while your income is above the basic exemption limit.

## Records to keep
Invoices, PayPal/Upwork statements, bank statements, FIRC/FIRA for each foreign receipt, your LUT acknowledgement if GST-registered, and receipts for business expenses. Check your Annual Information Statement (AIS) before filing.

## Gross vs net
Report the gross amount clients paid. Platform fees (for example Upwork's service fee) and PayPal fees are expenses. Under the presumptive scheme, expenses are already covered by the 50% deemed profit, so they are not deducted again.
